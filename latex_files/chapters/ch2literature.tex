\section{Research Objective}
The goal of this project is to analyze how bots have been detected in previous studies, implement some of those detection methods to the latest and largest dataset, Twibot-22, regarding X posts and accounts, and create our own bot in an attempt to avoid such detection methods to provide better insight as to how big of a problem bots really are.

It is important to note that creating a bot, analyzing the motivations behind creating bots, and following bot activity may involve ethical lapses such as the spread of misinformation. This can be due to many factors, such as deliberately spreading misinformation to push a narrative, known as disinformation. Another factor that is unavoidable, at least in terms of this project, is the risk that a GPT “hallucinates” and returns false information. A hallucination with respect to AI means that the information was presented as fact, even though the information is false. This was a very common occurrence in earlier GPT models, and although hallucinations have seen a significant downward trend since then, hallucinations can and still occur. Another ethical concern is regarding the use of social media apps and the privacy of its users’. Users interact on X on a daily basis and some of them have public accounts. It is important to maintain and respect the user's privacy, regardless if their accounts are public or not.

\section{Technical Approach}
We will survey a variety of different methods that have been used for bot detection before, such as ML classifiers \cite{MLClassifiers}, LLM Bot Guard \cite{LLMBotGuard}, and \ac{NLP} techniques \cite{MisinfoCampaigns}. We'll organize each method based on what detected best and when the detection occurred. Afterwards, we will use the detection methods on the latest dataset, Twibot-22, which is a GitHub repository created by other researchers who are specializing in the field of bot detection \cite{Twibot-22}. Once that data is consolidated, we will create our own X bot using the developer options that X allows its users and create an account from there. We will investigate methods on how to automate via GPTs since they now pass the Turing test and can imitate human responses the best. Afterwards, we will revisit the different methods of bot detection and test them against our own bot to see if it circumvents detection and report our findings.
